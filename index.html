ABOUT

Researching policy, institutions, and development

My research lies at the intersection of public policy and economics, with a particular interest in understanding how economic policies and institutions affect development outcomes.

I use quantitative methods and program evaluation techniques to investigate policy effectiveness, causal relationships, and distributional consequences. My work combines econometric analysis with institutional and policy frameworks to generate evidence that can inform better public decision-making.
